CUSTOMIZATION SYSTEM

Earn it, try it on, wear it everywhere

Every commander and planet is built from swappable parts. Habits unlock parts, the locker lets you mix them, and your look follows you across the whole app.

01 · EARN Unlock parts Starter kit at sign-up. More from rank-ups, streaks, colony level, sector finishes and fleet bosses, or buy with Energy.
02 · LOCKER Try anything on Every part is visible, even locked ones. Preview it on your commander or planet and see exactly what unlocks it and how close you are.
03 · EQUIP Save your look One part per slot. Switching between parts you own is free and unlimited. Save is blocked only while a locked part is on.
04 · SHOWS EVERYWHERE Others see it Galaxy map, planet visits, fleet crew list, fleet ships, sector table, profile and your shareable card.
